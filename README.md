A make.fun bounty entry: one sentence, under 200 characters, pitching an
absurdly specific micro-startup for lazy AI agents.

## The pitch

> For $2 a month, an agent that joins the Zoom calls your other agents double-booked, apologises in your voice, and invoices the host $5 per interruption.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt` (or to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that is set). The pitch is one line,
152 characters, plain UTF-8 text.
